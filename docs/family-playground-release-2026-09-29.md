# Family Playground

Open **Playground & Family Club** in the island footer, or the play-break banner inside Play & earn.

- Pet Playground: jump over logs and collect stars with an equipped companion, or a guest kitten.
- Rimba Boat Dash: steer between three lanes, collect stars and avoid rocks.
- Each round lasts 45 active seconds. Pause, background-tab pause and early exit are supported. Stars give 40 playground points; obstacles deduct 10, with a zero minimum. Scores never award Hero Coins or learning mastery.
- Each explorer has separate personal bests and completed-round counts. There is no public ranking, account discovery or chat.
- Family lighthouse project: finish 6 learning adventures/quizzes and 4 playground rounds. Either explorer can contribute. Counts begin with this update, have no deadline and cap at the target. The restored lighthouse remains visible on the island.
- Existing saves gain safe defaults for the new fields. New progress uses the existing local/cloud save path. New game assets are included in the offline cache.

Verification covers state normalization, reward separation, pause/completion/exit, offline launch, small-phone layout, learning contributions, persistent personal records and visible lighthouse state. Independent review found no release blockers. This update does not change the pending SMTP setup for public parent email delivery.
